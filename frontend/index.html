About

A resnet18 convolutional network, pretrained on ImageNet and fine-tuned in PyTorch on a 1,722-photo, five-breed dataset to spot ragdolls.

The model is exported to ONNX and runs entirely in your browser, so your photo never leaves your device.

This is part of a from-scratch ML project: first a hand-derived NumPy network, then an MLP baseline, then transfer learning. The project is still in progress, so the judge will not always be right. This page is the deployed result.
